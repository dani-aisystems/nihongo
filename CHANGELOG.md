# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-27

First public release.

- 168-day A1 curriculum in six stages, with weekly checks, stage assessments and a final A1 assessment.
- Lesson player with Learn, Listen, Practice, Apply, Recall and Challenge phases, in Quick, Standard and Deep modes.
- 13 exercise types, including romaji-to-kana typing, sentence tiles, branching dialogues, reading and listening passages.
- Spaced repetition with separate recognition and recall tracking, kana confusion drills and a mistake notebook.
- Practice, Progress and Library sections.
- Text-to-speech picks the clearest Japanese voice on the device. Single kana play slower so each sound is distinct.
- Practice and the Library only use lessons you've completed.
- Backup export and import.
